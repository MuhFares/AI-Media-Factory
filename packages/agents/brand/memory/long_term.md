# Brand Agent — Long-Term Memory

Durable knowledge that persists across runs. Vector-backed; linked to [packages/database](../../../database/README.md) and grounded in the [Brand Guidelines](../../../../memory/company/brand-guidelines.md).

## What is stored here

- **Ruling ledger.** Every APPROVE / HOLD / escalate decision, the asset it concerned, the rationale, and — filled in later — the production outcome (did an approved asset stay safe and perform; was a held asset correctly held).
- **Voice precedents.** Recurring on-voice and off-voice patterns, so the same phrasing is judged consistently across assets and brands.
- **Safety precedents.** Categories of content that triggered safety holds, forming a growing, checkable standard.
- **Per-brand nuance.** Voice and safety specifics that differ by owned brand.

## How it is used

At the start of each review the Brand agent loads relevant precedents so rulings stay consistent and do not drift. Outcomes are written back so the standard sharpens over time — this is Compounding Knowledge applied to brand governance, and it is what gives enterprise buyers an auditable trail.

## Retention

Durable and versioned. Nothing is deleted; superseded rulings are marked, not removed, preserving the audit history.
