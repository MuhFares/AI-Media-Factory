# Blog Templates

Blog and article content templates for the AI Media Factory platform. This folder holds reusable scaffolding for long-form written content, including article structure, frontmatter, and SEO scaffolding.

Contents are runtime-generated and git-ignored; this README and .gitkeep anchor the folder.

## Typical Contents

- Article structure and section outlines.
- Frontmatter templates for metadata.
- SEO scaffolding such as meta descriptions and keyword placeholders.
