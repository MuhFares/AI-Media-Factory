# Templates

Reusable content templates root for the AI Media Factory platform. This folder is the parent for platform-specific template collections used to standardize generated content across channels.

Contents are runtime-generated and git-ignored; this README and .gitkeep anchor the folder.

## Platform Subfolders

| Subfolder | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `youtube/` | YouTube-specific templates such as titles, descriptions, end-screens, and chapter formats. |
| `instagram/` | Instagram and Reels templates such as captions, hashtag sets, and cover formats. |
| `blog/` | Blog and article templates covering structure, frontmatter, and SEO scaffolding. |
| `email/` | Email and newsletter templates such as subject lines, layouts, and calls to action. |
