## Editorial promise

A short observational Egyptian-Arabic documentary about Cairo as lived experience—not a tourist landmark montage and not a quantified viral-trend claim.

## Working title and packaging

- **Title:** القاهرة كما يعيشها أهلها
- **Hook:** القاهرة مش بس الأهرامات… الحكاية الحقيقية بتبدأ في الشارع.
- **Format:** vertical short-form, approximately 25–35 seconds after final narration timing
- **Audience:** Egyptian and Arabic-speaking viewers interested in local city culture
- **Tone:** warm, observational, grounded, lightly cinematic

## Egyptian-Arabic narration draft

القاهرة مش بس الأهرامات ولا الصور اللي بنشوفها في البروشورات.
القاهرة صوت الميكروباص، ورائحة القهوة، ولافتة قديمة لسه بتحكي عن المكان.
هي ناس رايحة شغلها، وناس واقفة تستنى، وصاحب كافيه عارف زباينه واحد واحد.
وسط الزحمة، دايمًا فيه لحظة هدوء صغيرة: شباك مفتوح، شمس على مبنى قديم، أو ضحكة طالعة من آخر الشارع.
دي القاهرة… مش بس مدينة بنزورها، دي مدينة ناس بتعيشها كل يوم.

## Suggested visual beats

1. **0–5s — Hook:** accepted Cairo street visual; on-screen title and safe-zone Arabic caption.
2. **5–12s — Movement:** traffic, microbus, walking, and street signs; use only semantically matching local or newly approved visuals.
3. **12–20s — Human texture:** café detail, hands, storefront, or ordinary work moment; do not imply a specific event without evidence.
4. **20–28s — Quiet contrast:** old building, window light, or a brief pause in the street.
5. **28–35s — Close:** title/brand lockup and the line “مدينة بنعيشها كل يوم”.

## Evidence and guardrails

- Research supports an editorial/cultural angle around streets, cafés, signs, buildings, and movement; it does not establish a numerical viral ranking.
- No Al Ahly/Enppi match footage or Mawlid/event-specific imagery is required for this selected package.
- Do not use FLUX-derived `output/canonical-wan/scene-001..003` as current production evidence.
- The accepted Wan artifact is a single scene. It may support a single-scene proof or an edit fixture, but it cannot prove multi-scene continuity by itself.
- VoiceTuT is the intended Egyptian narration path. A live VoiceTuT call remains a provider action and is not included in this local preparation.
