## Selected topic

**Working topic:** Cairo as lived experience: the everyday city beyond landmark tourism.

**Editorial angle:** A short, observational Egyptian-Arabic documentary that treats ordinary street life as the story: movement, work, cafés, signs, and small moments of calm. The piece must avoid claiming a trend statistic that the sources do not establish; “trend” here means current cultural/editorial interest, not a quantified viral ranking.
