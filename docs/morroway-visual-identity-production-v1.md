## 2. Logo Exploration Contract

**Total:** max 6 materially distinct concepts (NOT 20 minor variants). Each must differ in structure.

### Route A — Threshold Arc (primary production route, max 3)
Explore: passage, threshold, horizon, opening, transition, restrained arc geometry.
Must support: wordmark integration, avatar crop, animation/reveal, monochrome, watermark.
Must NOT become: generic portal logo, generic sunrise, letter C, browser/app icon, sci-fi portal cliché.

### Route B — Abstract M Passage (challenger, max 3)
Explore: M-based geometry, converging paths, forward movement, dimensional passage, negative space.
Must support: same as Route A. The M must work visually without explanation.
Must NOT become: generic finance logo, mountain M, McKinsey-like geometry, crypto token, gaming clan logo.

### Route C — Layered Dimension
Supporting/reference only. Not a primary finalist unless owner reauthorizes.

## 3. Logo Hard Constraints

Forbidden clichés: camera, play triangle, robot, AI sparkle, literal hourglass, castle, sword, parchment, globe, infinity symbol, literal doorway illustration. Avoid overly intricate marks. Minimum: 32px avatar legibility, dark + light, monochrome, watermark, motion.

## 4. Logo Evaluation Criteria (deterministic owner-review matrix)

1 Distinctiveness · 2 Memorability · 3 Morroway strategic fit · 4 Threshold concept fit · 5 Historical + Fantasy balance · 6 Simplicity · 7 32px avatar legibility · 8 Monochrome performance · 9 Wordmark integration · 10 Motion potential · 11 Watermark suitability · 12 International neutrality · 13 Arabic/bilingual compatibility · 14 Merchandise potential · 15 Cliché avoidance. No automatic winner; owner decides.

## 5. Color Refinement Contract

Preserve ABYSSAL CORE (#0B0F1A, #1E2A3A, #4A5568 — exploratory, NOT final). Explore max 3 accent treatments (conceptually: restrained warm light; spectral/cinematic highlight; luminous passage accent — names/colors not final).
Avoid: generic neon cyberpunk, purple/blue AI SaaS aesthetic, excessive gradients, oversaturated fantasy palettes.
Final system must support: DARK MODE, LIGHT MODE, VIDEO, THUMBNAILS, WEB, SOCIAL GRAPHICS, SUBTITLES, ACCESSIBILITY. Final HEX requires owner approval (FINAL_PALETTE = PENDING).

## 6. Typography Selection Contract

Shortlist max 3 Latin systems + corresponding Arabic-compatible strategies. Evaluate: brand character, mobile readability, subtitle readability, headline impact, multilingual support, licensing practicality, open-source/commercial availability, video rendering. Do NOT distribute font files.

## 7. Graphic Language Contract

Future components: threshold framing, arcs, directional lines, depth layers, light passage, atmospheric contrast, restrained gradients, dimensional reveals. Usable across Historical POV and Fantasy Storytelling. Avoid excessive fantasy ornamentation.

## 8. AI Visual Production Quality

Required: cinematic realism, controlled lighting, clean anatomy, accurate faces, coherent perspective, natural textures, controlled color grading, intentional composition, historical credibility where applicable, fantasy imagination without clutter. Reject generic AI look, oversaturated fantasy, random sci-fi.

## 9. Social Identity Production

Future deliverables: profile/avatar mark (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok), YouTube banner direction, video watermark, end-card identity, thumbnail/cover framework. Display name Morroway everywhere. Do not create platform accounts.

## 10. Bilingual Readiness

Anticipate English + Arabic/RTL without two unrelated identities. Logo symbol remains shared; wordmark/typography may adapt. Arabic must stay recognizably Morroway.

## 11. Visual Reference Research Recommendation

**VISUAL_REFERENCE_RESEARCH_REQUIRED = YES** (bounded, before asset generation): examine cinematic title design, premium entertainment identities, editorial storytelling systems, motion branding, historical/fantasy visual language — without copying existing brands. Rationale: logo routes need cliché-avoidance evidence (portal/sunrise/finance-M risks) and premium benchmarks; research is read-only and does not produce assets.

## 12. Production Tool / Agent Architecture

- CREATIVE DIRECTION = AMF creative/brand agent (strategy + judgment)
- IMAGE / LOGO GENERATION = visual generation capability/tool (execution only)
- VALIDATION = brand QA + deterministic checks (15-criteria matrix, cliché scan, scale/monochrome tests) where possible
- OWNER = final visual approval
- OpenCode/Codex = engineering/operator layer (must NOT substitute for creative agents)

No permanent image-generation provider/tool is chosen in this step. Future tooling must support: image/logo generation, iteration, transparent backgrounds, high resolution, style consistency, reproducibility/reference support, commercial-usage clarity, cost tracking.

## 13. Governance

Owner approval required for: final logo route, final HEX palette, font families, tagline, channel-identity change, sub-brand, series spinout, endorsement change. Team operates thumbnails/captions/episode graphics within rules.
