## 1. Purpose

This document defines the **formal relationship** between the 7 naming layers that were previously conflated. It answers `WHAT "AI MEDIA FACTORY / AMF" IS` and `WHAT "MORROWAY / REMNARA" WOULD BE` without selecting a final brand, registering a company, or creating handles.

It prevents future conflicts such as `Company: Morroway / Brand: Morroway Media / Instagram: Morroway Stories` unless explicitly approved as a deliberate architecture.

## 3. Layer Definitions

### Layer 1 — Legal Entity

**Purpose:** Formal registered company / establishment name.

**Current Status:** `NOT_FINALIZED`

**Policy:**
- Do NOT lock legal name now.
- Legal name may vary by jurisdiction (e.g., UAE, UK, US).
- Must be validated against commercial registration availability, trademark availability, corporate structure, ownership/tax requirements.
- Do NOT treat `AI Media Factory` as legally verified.

**Examples (non-binding, not selected):** `AMF Digital Media LLC`, `AI Media Factory LLC`.

### Layer 2 — Operating / Corporate Identity

**Identity:** `AI Media Factory` / `AMF` (preferred current)

**Purpose:**
- Company operating identity
- Internal business identity
- Technology / media production organization
- Owner of workflows, systems, future brand portfolio
- Business-facing identity for partnerships, B2B, recruitment, investor decks

**Policy:** `AMF` is **NOT** necessarily the audience-facing entertainment brand. It is the **Corporate / Operating Parent** that owns the consumer brand(s).

### Layer 3 — Consumer Master Brand

**Current Finalists:** `Morroway`, `Remnara` (one will be selected)

**Purpose:**
- Primary public storytelling identity
- Audience-recognizable media property
- Umbrella for both pillars (`Historical POV Narration` + `AI-Generated Fantasy Storytelling`)
- Brand equity accumulation

**Policy:**
- One final name will be selected later (`FINAL_BRAND_DECISION_V1_MORROWAY_VS_REMNARA`).
- Naming is a separate owner-approved phase `BRAND_AND_CHANNEL_NAMING_V1` (not yet final).
- No final brand is selected in this document.

### Layer 4 — Channel / Page Display Name

**Default Principle:** Use the **Consumer Master Brand consistently** across platforms.

**Policy — `DISPLAY_NAME_POLICY`:**
- Preferred: `Morroway` on Instagram, YouTube, TikTok (no suffix).
- Alternative only if true language expansion requires separate channel identities:
  - `Morroway Arabic`
  - `Morroway Español`
- **Forbidden by default:** `Morroway TV`, `Morroway Stories`, `Morroway AI`, `Morroway Media` — unless explicitly approved as a deliberate architecture (e.g., distinct product line).

**Rule:** `ONE CONSUMER MASTER BRAND + CONSISTENT DISPLAY NAME + HANDLE VARIATIONS ONLY WHEN NECESSARY`.

### Layer 5 — Social Handle / Username

**Preferred:** `@consumerbrand` (e.g., `@morroway` / `@remnara`)

**Fallback Hierarchy (Tiered):**
1.  `@brand` (Tier 1 — preferred)
2.  `@brandstories` (Tier 2)
3.  `@brandmedia` (Tier 3)
4.  `@brandworld` (Tier 4)
5.  `@getbrand` or `@brandhq` only where appropriate (Tier 5 — last resort)

**Acceptable:** `@brand`, `@brandstories`, `@brandmedia`, `@brandworld`, `@getbrand`/`@brandhq` (when brandable).

**Discouraged:** `@brand_2026`, `@official_brand_real`, `@brand123`, `@brand_tv_official`, `@brand__`, excessively long or confusing suffixes.

**Forbidden patterns:** `@brand_2026`, `@official_brand_real`, `@brand123`, `@brand_tv_official` (low-equity, date-stamped, or misleading).

**Policy:** Handle variation **must NOT redefine the brand**. A handle is an access handle, not a rebrand.

### Layer 6 — Content Series / Franchises

**Purpose:** Names for specific repeatable content formats / shows / story worlds.

**Policy:**
- A series name is created **only** when it has: repeatable format, recognizable audience proposition, multiple episodes, durable identity, strategic value.
- Series are **children** of the Consumer Master Brand, not companies or master brands unless later deliberately spun out.
- Examples conceptually (not selected): Historical POV series, Fantasy storytelling series, future thematic franchises.

**Examples from Council Strategy (not series names):** `Historical POV Moments`, `Emotional Fantasy Vignettes`, `Mythology Quick Hits`.

### Layer 7 — Internal Platform / Product Identity

**Identity:** `AMF Control Platform`

**Purpose:**
- Orchestration, agent operations, workflows, production control, approvals, analytics, provenance, cost, publishing operations.

**Policy:**
- `AMF Control Platform` is **NOT** the public consumer brand, not a social channel brand, not a content series.
- It is `internal / operating technology platform` (potential future B2B productization requires separate naming review if commercialized externally).
- Must not be confused with `Morroway`/`Remnara` consumer brand.

## 5. AMF Role Evaluation

**Question:** Should AMF be A) Consumer-facing master brand, B) Corporate parent only, C) Endorsed parent `Morroway by AMF`, D) Invisible parent?

**Decision:** **B) Corporate / Operating Parent only** (preferred hypothesis).

**Rationale:**
- CEO strategy positions `AI Media Factory / AMF` as the operating/technology identity that owns the consumer storytelling brand, not the story itself.
- Consumer research shows `Morroway`/`Remnara` are invented, globally pronounceable, and not tied to `AMF`'s tech/production connotation; an `AMF`-led consumer brand would narrow emotional storytelling.
- Scalability: `AMF` as parent allows future `Consumer Brand B`, B2B services, and `AMF Control Platform` without rebranding the consumer brand.

**`Morroway by AMF` / `Remnara by AMF`:** Not assumed. Requires strategic justification (e.g., corporate credibility for B2B partnerships). Not the consumer default.

## 6. Endorsement Policy

**When AMF may appear publicly:**

- Website footer: `Morroway — An AI Media Factory brand` or `© AI Media Factory`
- Legal pages, business partnerships, investor decks, recruitment, B2B services, `About` pages, copyright notices.

**Endorsement level for consumer-facing channels:** `LIGHT` (not `PROMINENT`, not `NONE`).

- **NONE:** No AMF mention on consumer channels (risks losing corporate credibility).
- **LIGHT:** Small footer `An AI Media Factory brand` / `© AI Media Factory` (preferred).
- **VISIBLE:** Explicit `Morroway by AMF` lockup on profile.
- **PROMINENT:** `AMF` as primary consumer brand (rejected per §5).

**Policy:** Consumer channels show `Morroway`/`Remnara` as hero; `AMF` appears only in light endorsement contexts (footer, About, legal). No `Morroway by AMF` as default display name.

## 9. Future Scalability

Architecture must support:

- More than one consumer brand (e.g., second brand after Morroway/Remnara)
- More than one channel (Reels → Shorts → TikTok is `PHASED_PORTFOLIO`, not a single page)
- More than one language (`Morroway Arabic` only if true language expansion requires separate channel)
- New content verticals, B2B services, software/platform products, licensing, merchandising, partnerships

Do not design the company around a single Instagram page. The hierarchy above already provides `Future Consumer Brand B` and `Future Products / Media Properties` as siblings.
