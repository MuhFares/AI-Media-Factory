# Assets / Logos

Brand logos and mark variations for use across produced content and platform surfaces.

## What belongs here

- Primary and secondary logo marks.
- Variations by color, orientation, and clear-space requirements.
- Usage records and any associated brand guidance.

## Binaries are git-ignored placeholders

Logo binary files are git-ignored placeholders. Actual logo files are stored in the platform's asset store, not committed to version control. This folder is anchored by its README.

## Naming conventions

- Name variants by mark, treatment, and format, in kebab-case.
- Keep light and dark treatments clearly distinguished.
