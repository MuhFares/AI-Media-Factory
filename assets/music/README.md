# Assets / Music

Background music and audio beds used in produced media.

## What belongs here

- Music tracks and audio beds cleared for platform use.
- License records defining permitted use for each track.

## Binaries are git-ignored placeholders

Audio binary files are git-ignored placeholders. Actual music files are stored in the platform's asset store, not committed to version control. This folder is anchored by its README.

## Naming conventions

- Organize by mood, genre, or intended use.
- Include tempo and duration in the track name where useful.
